How Touch Grass Bingo Works

Turn your daily walks into an engaging scavenger hunt in three easy steps.

🚶‍♂️
Step 01

Step Outside

Head out into your garden, street, balcony, or neighborhood park. A new 3×3 board refreshes every midnight.

📷
Step 02

Spot & Photograph

Spot items on your board like puddles, yellow leaves, birds, or mushrooms. Tap any tile to snap a photo.

🤖
Step 03

AI Match & Bingo!

Our vision AI verifies your photo in memory. Line up 3 items to shout BINGO, or find all 9 to become Garden Master!

⚡ ZERO COMPROMISES

Built for Mindful Exploration

Designed with privacy, local biodiversity, and seamless performance at its heart.

🧠

Zero-Shot Vision AI

Powered by open-weight OpenCLIP ViT-B-32 with cosine text embeddings. Runs fast on CPU with zero cloud dependencies.

🌴

Sri Lanka Biodiversity

Tailored nature items including coconut trees, jackfruit leaves, millipedes, and rain puddles in both English and Sinhala (සිංහල).

🔄

Cannot Find? Swap It!

No banana tree or snail shell in your area? Easily swap any uncompleted tile with another item from the master nature list.

🔒

100% In-Memory Privacy

Photos are compressed right inside your browser canvas, evaluated in RAM, and immediately discarded. Never saved to disk.

🔥

Daily Outdoor Streaks

Keep your daily fire glowing. Build a healthy routine of connecting with fresh air and green surroundings every single day.

📲

Offline-Ready PWA

Install to your home screen like a native app. Works fast and smooth on walks even when mobile coverage is weak.

🍃

Scientific studies show that just 20 minutes spent in green natural spaces significantly lowers cortisol, restores mental clarity, and lifts mood. Step outside and let the natural world surprise you!
